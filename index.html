Our Story

A Legacy of
Culinary Excellence

Founded in 2018 by Executive Chef Marcus Laurent, Aurum represents the pinnacle of modern gastronomy. Every dish is a canvas where seasonal ingredients from local farms meet innovative techniques from around the world.

Our philosophy is simple — honor the ingredients, respect the craft, and create moments that linger long after the last bite.
